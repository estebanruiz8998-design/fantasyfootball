All research gathered **4 September 2026** via web search. Direct page fetches
were blocked by the network egress policy in the build environment, so figures
come from search-result summaries rather than scraped tables. Where a number is
quoted below, it is quoted because a source stated it explicitly.

Verify anything time-sensitive before drafting — particularly injury statuses
and your own platform's ADP.
